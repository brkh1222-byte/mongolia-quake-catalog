**D20 rules and sources.** The literature gives no coordinates for the Hangay or the Gobi-Altai, and does not define a dome "interior" versus "margin" (Meltzer et al. 2019 place the South Hangay, Bayanhongor and Egiin Davaa faults "within the Hangay"). So the regions are built from reproducible rules tied to published numbers:

- **hangay_interior** (102,000 km², 50 HD stations): the largest connected area where ETOPO1 elevation, smoothed over 50 km, is ≥ **2000 m**, after a 40 km morphological opening, clipped to the HD footprint. *Why 2000 m:* Meltzer et al. (2019, p. 1364): "the broad domal Hangay upland (∼200,000 km2) … Elevations within the Hangay average 2500 m, approximately 1000 m above the regional trend." 2000 m is the half-height of the dome: midway between the regional level (~1500 m) and the dome average (2500 m). It encloses about half of the published upland area, so it is the high core. *Pre-declared sensitivity:* repeat H2b with thresholds 1800 m and 2200 m (`python src/make_regions.py --thresh X --no-write`).
- **hangay_margin** (230,000 km², 22 HD stations outside the interior): HD station convex hull buffered 0.3°, minus the interior. Meltzer et al. (2019, p. 1366): "The 72-station Hangay dome subarray is ∼500 × 600 km." It contains the Bulnay fault "along the northern edge of the Hangay" (p. 1371), the "Valley of the Lakes, a low-lying sedimentary basin south of the Hangay" (p. 1366) and the southern slopes with the South Hangay fault system (Walker et al. 2008, §3.4: "extends for ∼350 km along the southern slopes of the Hangay"; excerpt only, not checked against the PDF).
- **khovsgol** (46,000 km², 26 HV stations): HV station convex hull buffered 0.3°. Meltzer et al. (2019, p. 1366): "The 26-station Khövsgöl subarray is 200 × 200 km … deployed across the Khövsgöl and Darkhad rifts". The polygon lies inside the study box of Ganbaatar & Sereenendorj (2024, p. 52): 48–52.5°N, 97.0–102.5°E. The Busingol basin (Logatchev 1993) lies west of the array and is outside the polygon.
- **gobi_altai** (59,000 km², 14 AT stations): AT station convex hull buffered 0.3°. Meltzer et al. (2019, p. 1366): "The 14-station Gobi-Altai subarray is ∼250 by 150 km … at the intersection of the Mongolian Altai and Gobi-Altai ranges". It covers the western Bogd fault but **not** the whole 260-km 1957 rupture (Rizza et al. 2011), whose eastern part lies in hangay_margin.
- Regions are limited to station footprints because depths are only reliable inside the network. Where polygons overlap, the later one wins (margin < interior < khovsgol < gobi_altai), so events are labelled by geography.
- DEM: ETOPO1 (NOAA ERDDAP dataset `etopo180`), 1 arc-minute.
