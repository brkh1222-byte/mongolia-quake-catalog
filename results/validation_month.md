# Pilot validation: pilot_2013-01

**What this is:** a methods check of the full pipeline on one month (2013-01-01 to 2013-02-01, exclusive) of the Hangay subarray (72 stations operating). It is not a scientific result: Mc, b and D90 from one month are dominated by sampling noise and are reported only to show that the code works end to end.
